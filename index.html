Significance to the Community

Why lightweight, local AI matters

Low Barrier to Entry

Anyone with a browser can try and learn from the model without installing software, configuring environments, or paying for cloud compute.

Privacy-First Inference

Images are processed locally in the browser session rather than sent to a custom backend, making experimentation safe and straightforward.

Open & Iterative

Hosted on GitHub, the project invites classmates, teammates, and community members to test edge cases and improve data quality together.

Principles of Design

Clarity, structure, and calm focus

Hierarchy & Balance

Serif display headings establish editorial character while a two-column layout balances visual input on the left with real-time probability feedback on the right.

Contrast & Proximity

Crisp dark typography on a warm neutral canvas preserves readability, while tight grouping links each label directly to its confidence meter.

Repetition & Whitespace

Consistent card framing, rounded controls, and generous spacing keep the interface quiet so the live prediction results stay front and center.
